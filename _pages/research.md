My research focuses on **natural language processing** and **machine learning**, with an emphasis on understanding and improving large language and vision-language models.

## Research Interests

### LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
I study methods for improving the reasoning capabilities of large language models, including the scalable synthesis of verifiable reasoning data and learning strategies that extend beyond individual reasoning tasks.

### Hallucination in Vision-Language Models
I investigate why vision-language models hallucinate and where their perception capabilities become bottlenecks, including in chart-understanding settings.

### LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability
I study internal representations related to truthfulness and hallucination, with the goal of making model behavior more reliable and interpretable.

## Research Expertise

- Natural language processing and machine learning
- Large language model reasoning and reinforcement learning
- Vision-language models and chart understanding
- Hallucination detection and mitigation
- LLM truthfulness and interpretability
- Verifiable reasoning-data synthesis
- Foundation-model evaluation and inner-representation analysis
